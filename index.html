THE PROBLEM

Energy consumption is increasing.

Electricity consumption continues to increase as cities, buildings and public spaces become more dependent on electrical systems.

At the same time, millions of footsteps happen every day in schools, malls, stations and public areas.

Wasted Mechanical Energy

The energy created by people's movement is normally lost.

OUR SOLUTION

Every Footstep Can Make A Difference.

Our system captures mechanical energy from footsteps and converts it into electrical energy.
